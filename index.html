About
"The most honest writing happens when the writer stops performing and begins to simply look."

Nick Marsan is a fiction writer and MFA candidate at Western Connecticut State University whose work explores memory, redemption, and the quiet weight of ordinary life. His stories and essays have appeared in Mobius Boulevard and Afterwards, an anthology from the Fordham Veterans Writing Workshops.

He teaches creative writing to senior citizens at the Westport Center for Senior Activities, a practice that has taught him as much about storytelling as any classroom. He is at work on his first novel, Things We Want to Remember — a story about memory and what we reach for when it starts to slip.

He writes a newsletter on the craft of fiction: on revision, on the reading that feeds writing, on the long and unglamorous work of learning to tell a story that is true.
